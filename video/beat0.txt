Running a household is a rolling list of small decisions that never stops. Have the kids outgrown their shoes? Is a gift sorted for the anniversary in two weeks? Did the grocery reorder go out? None of these is hard, but doing them well means remembering last year, checking today's prices, and staying inside a budget. Household Chief of Staff is a background agent that carries that list. Once a day it sweeps the family's data, checks every idea against a budget, and emails you one decision at a time: approve, or reject.
